# Viktron Health — Precision Dementia Care AI Suite

> **Restoring cognitive dignity through local intelligence and empathetic design.**

Viktron Health is a high-fidelity cognitive assurance platform designed to assist individuals living with dementia and support their care teams. By combining custom hardware and intuitive software, we bridge the gap between patient independence and clinical oversight.

Our platform operates on a zero-trust, privacy-first architecture where sensitive patient data and real-time processing remain strictly local.

---

## 👁️ The Care Ecosystem

The Viktron Health platform is comprised of two integrated, human-centric form factors powered by a unified local intelligence layer:

### 1. AuraPath™ Smart Glasses (Social Memory Prosthetic)
AuraPath is a lightweight, non-stigmatizing assistive wearable (weighing under 38g) designed to restore confidence and social fluidity for individuals experiencing memory impairment.

*   **Sub-Second Facial Recognition**: Local optical sensors detect and match facial vectors in under 400ms, immediately identifying family and care staff.
*   **Contextual Audio Cues (Bone Conduction)**: Whisper-quiet bone-conduction transducers deliver relationship context (e.g., *"Your daughter, Sarah"*) directly to the user's inner ear, maintaining natural eye contact and communication flows.
*   **Optical Display Array**: A transparent micro-OLED waveguide projects clean, non-obtrusive waypoint prompts, facial vectors, and safety alerts directly into the wearer’s field of view.
*   **On-Device Neural Engine**: All computation runs locally via an 8-bit quantized edge inference processor, ensuring immediate processing with zero outbound network requirements.
*   **Ambient Safety Triage**: Continuous monitoring for physical hazards, vital signs, and light changes to proactively keep the wearer safe.

### 2. AuraGuide™ Copilot (Caregiver Dashboard)
AuraGuide is a mobile-first dashboard built specifically for family members and clinical care teams, functioning as a real-time medical copilot.

*   **Integrated Vitals & Medication Triage**: Real-time logging of vital signs and safety status synced directly from AuraPath.
*   **Electronic Health Record (EHR) Sync**: Native compatibility with major clinical systems via HL7 FHIR protocols, keeping medical files synchronized and updated.
*   **Smart Medication Checks**: Automatic validation of medication schedules and potential interactions.
*   **Caregiver Workflow Automation**: A voice-first interface that enables caregivers to log updates, coordinate check-ins, and receive clinical assistance on the go.

## 🛡️ Our Core Pillars

### 🔒 Zero-Trust Privacy & Local Intelligence
In dementia care, privacy is paramount. By utilizing custom edge-processors (NPUs) on AuraPath, we eliminate the need for continuous cloud sync. Facial data, locations, and personal profiles remain encrypted locally on the device, ensuring zero leakage of patient identity.

### 🤝 Empathy-Driven Design
We believe technology should get out of the way of human relationships. We replaced blinking screens and obtrusive speakers with bone-conduction audio and transparent waveguide displays so that patients can interact naturally with their loved ones, free from the stigma of traditional assistive hardware.

### 🏥 Seamless Clinical Integration
Our architecture communicates securely using standards-based medical endpoints. Vitals logs, compliance checks, and emergency alerts are structured as standard FHIR resources, ensuring medical providers have accurate, actionable data when they need it most.
