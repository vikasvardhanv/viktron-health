AuraPath Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026 · Operated by Viktron Health

AuraPath is a smart-glasses companion app. It helps a person recognize familiar people, understand their surroundings, and get reminders by voice. This policy explains what the app collects, who receives it, and the choices you have.

1. What AuraPath collects

  • Camera images. When you ask Aura to identify a person, object, or pill, or while a voice session is active, the glasses take photos. The app sends these photos to the vision service to produce a description.
  • Voice audio. While a voice session is active, the microphone audio goes to the voice service so Aura can answer. The app does not listen when no session is active.
  • People you save. Name, relationship, optional notes, and a reference photo for each person you ask Aura to remember.
  • Identification history. The label, description, and time of each identification.
  • Reminders. The text and time of each reminder you set.
  • Emergency contact and settings. The contact name and phone number you enter, and your choices such as Whisper Mode and Calm Mode.
  • Glasses status. Device name, battery level, and media counts. Bluetooth is used only to talk to your glasses.
  • Account information. Your name, and your email address if you create an account.
  • Medicines from Medhavn. AuraPath and Medhavn share one account. AuraPath reads your Medhavn medicine list and dose records so a pill check can say whether a dose is due. When you confirm a dose in AuraPath, it records the dose in Medhavn.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to run the features you ask for: identify people and objects, hold a voice conversation, set reminders, and reach your emergency contact. We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising.

3. Who receives it

  • Viktron Health AI servers. Photos and voice audio are processed on servers that Viktron Health operates. We do not send them to a third-party AI company. The servers delete the photo and audio after they reply. A photo is kept only when the app saves it to your identification history.
  • Supabase. Our database and file host. It stores your saved people, reference photos, identification history and its photos, reminders, and settings. Access rules limit each record to its owner.
  • Your emergency contact. The app opens your phone’s call or message screen. The app does not send a message by itself. You or a caregiver must tap Send.

If you turn on on-device mode and download the on-device model, image identification can run on the phone without sending the photo to our servers.

4. Face recognition

AuraPath compares a new photo with the reference photos you saved. It does this only for people you chose to add. It does not search for strangers and does not build a public database.

5. Your choices

  • Turn off cloud backup of known people in Settings.
  • Choose whether transcripts are stored, and whether names are removed from exports.
  • Remove any saved person from the People screen.
  • Delete your account and all stored data in the app: Settings › Delete account. This also deletes your Medhavn account, because the two apps share one login. You can also email the address below.

6. Retention and security

We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account. Data is sent over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Health information

AuraPath is a memory-support companion. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor any condition. Do not rely on it in an emergency. Call your local emergency number.

8. Children

AuraPath is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.

10. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support@viktronhealth.com.
