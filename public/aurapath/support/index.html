AuraPath Support

AuraPath is operated by Viktron Health.

Need help with the app, your glasses, or your account? Email us and we will reply as soon as we can: support@viktronhealth.com.

Common questions

How do I pair my glasses?

Turn on Bluetooth and allow the permission when asked. Open Settings in AuraPath and tap the glasses pairing option. Pick your device from the list.

Aura does not answer.

Tap the microphone to start a session. Make sure microphone permission is on and the phone has an internet connection.

Aura cannot see anything.

Make sure the glasses are connected and charged. Photo capture uses the glasses camera and works on iPhone.

How do I set my emergency contact?

Open Settings, enter the contact name and phone number, and tap Save contact. The SOS button on the home screen then calls that number.

How do I remove a saved person?

Open People, select the person, and tap Remove.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us from the address on your account and ask for deletion. We will confirm and delete your saved people, photos, history, reminders, and settings.

Safety

AuraPath is a memory-support companion and not a medical device. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
